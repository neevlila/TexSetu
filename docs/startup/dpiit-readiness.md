# DPIIT Startup India Recognition & Compliance — TexSetu

## 1. DPIIT Eligibility & Startup India Qualification

TexSetu satisfies all statutory criteria set forth by the **Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT)** under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India:

1. **Entity Structure**: Formed as a Private Limited Company (TexSetu Technologies Private Limited) under the Companies Act, 2013.
2. **Tenure**: Incorporating within the eligible 10-year period from the date of registration.
3. **Turnover Cap**: Projected turnover remains well below the statutory ₹100 Crore annual ceiling during initial years.
4. **Innovation & Scalability Mandate**: TexSetu introduces an innovative business model and software product—the **Digital Production Lot Passport**—which transforms unorganized, offline MSME job-work into an auditable digital workflow with significant potential for domestic employment generation.

---

## 2. Key Statutory Benefits & Application Strategy

### 2.1 Section 80-IAC Income Tax Exemption
- **3-Year Tax Holiday**: TexSetu is eligible to apply to the Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB) for a 100% tax holiday on corporate profits for three consecutive assessment years out of its first ten years, maximizing reinvestment into software development and ground operations.

### 2.2 Section 56(2)(viib) "Angel Tax" Exemption
- Exemption from angel tax provisions on investments received from domestic angel investors and accredited startup syndicates above fair market value (FMV).

### 2.3 Fast-Track Patent & Trademark Processing
- Access to an **80% rebate on patent application fees** and a **50% rebate on trademark filings**, along with expedited examination through government-facilitated IP panels.

### 2.4 Relaxed Public Procurement Norms
- Exemption from prior turnover and experience requirements in government textile tenders (e.g., procurement of uniforms for state police, armed forces, and Indian Railways) under the Public Procurement Policy for MSEs.
