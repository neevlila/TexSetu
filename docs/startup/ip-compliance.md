## 2. Trademark Strategy (Classes 35 & 42)

TexSetu files for Wordmark and Device/Logo protection with the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (CGPDTM), India:
- **Trade Mark Class 35**: Advertising; business management; organization and administration of commercial affairs; computerized online B2B marketplace ordering services in the field of textile job-work and fabric manufacturing.
- **Trade Mark Class 42**: Scientific and technological services and research and design relating thereto; design and development of computer hardware and software; Software as a Service (SaaS) featuring software for tracking textile production lots, quotation comparison, and quality management.

---

## 3. Copyright Protection of Software Codebase

Under the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, computer software source code and object code are protected as "literary works".
- **Source Code Deposit**: Filing copyright registration for the core Spring Boot service layer, entity state machines, and Thymeleaf visual layouts.
- **Assignment of IP**: Strict Employee and Contractor Invention Assignment Agreements ensuring all copyright in created source code is irrevocably assigned to TexSetu Technologies Private Limited.

## 4. Legal Compliance & Commercial Terms of Service

### 4.1 Terms of Service (ToS) & Disclaimer of Physical Guarantees
- **Platform Intermediary Status**: Under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000, TexSetu operates as an intermediary platform connecting independent commercial entities.
- **Explicit Quality Disclaimer**: The platform explicitly disclaims warranties regarding physical fabric defects. Uploaded photographs and entered inspection notes constitute user-submitted evidence rather than certified laboratory assurances.

### 4.2 Non-Circumvention Covenants
- Platform terms include a standard 12-month commercial non-circumvention clause for introductory connections facilitated via TexSetu RFQs, backed by dispute de-platforming sanctions.

### 4.3 Data Protection & Privacy Compliance
- Fully compliant with the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023. Explicit user consent is gathered during registration for business profile publishing, GSTIN tax validation, and transactional notifications.
