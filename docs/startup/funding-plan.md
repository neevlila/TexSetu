### Detailed Expenditure Breakdown
1. **Ground Field Operations & Mill Verification (40% - ₹1,00,00,000)**:
   - Deployment of a 6-person ground merchant success team in Surat to onboard and physically verify GIDC dyeing and printing mills.
   - On-site onboarding desks placed in major wholesale markets (STM, Millennium Market).
2. **Software Engineering & Product Architecture (30% - ₹75,00,000)**:
   - Compensation for 3 Senior Full-Stack Java Engineers, 1 Product Designer, and 1 QA Lead.
   - Cloud hosting, database backups, security audits, and continuous CI/CD pipelines.
3. **Marketing & Trade Association Partnerships (18% - ₹45,00,000)**:
   - Formal co-sponsorships with South Gujarat Chamber of Commerce and Industry (SGCCI) and Federation of Surat Textile Traders Association (FOSTTA).
   - Vernacular (Gujarati/Hindi) video walkthroughs, print brochures, and digital trade campaigns.
4. **Legal, Compliance & Working Capital Reserve (12% - ₹30,00,000)**:
   - DPIIT startup compliance, trademark filings, contracts drafting, and 12-month operational runway reserve.

## 3. Investor ROI & Exit Pathways

1. **Strategic Acquisition by Supply Chain Majors**: Logical acquirers include high-growth B2B supply chain platforms seeking deep vertical integration into raw fabric processing (e.g., Zetwerk, Fashinza, Udaan).
2. **Acquisition by Enterprise ERP or Financial Giants**: Enterprise giants (e.g., Tally Solutions, Reliance Retail / JioMart B2B) seeking transaction data and merchant payment flow across Indian MSME clusters.
3. **Long-Term IPO Path**: Self-sustaining, dividend-yielding marketplace reaching profitability on transaction take-rates across India's $28B job-work sector.
