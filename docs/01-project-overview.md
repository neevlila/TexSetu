# 01 — Project Overview: TEXSETU
**B2B Textile Job-Work and Manufacturing Marketplace**

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**TexSetu** ("Textile Bridge") is a specialized cloud-based B2B e-commerce and manufacturing workflow marketplace designed specifically for the decentralized textile processing ecosystem of **Surat, Gujarat, India**.

Unlike standard consumer retail e-commerce platforms that sell finished consumer garments, TexSetu is an industrial marketplace engineered around the complex lifecycle of outsourced textile job-work: **fabric dyeing, chemical processing, rotary & digital printing, computerized embroidery, and garment stitching/finishing**.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
TexSetu connects fabric traders, clothing manufacturers, and textile brands with verified job-work processing facilities through:
1. **Capability-Based Discovery**: Transparent matching by verified machinery, GIDC locations, indicative daily capacities, and minimum order quantities (MOQ).
2. **Structured Quotation Comparison**: Multi-provider bidding allowing buyers to evaluate unit rates, turnaround timelines, and wastage tolerances side-by-side.
3. **Atomic Single-Quote Acceptance**: Transaction-safe state transitions eliminating accidental double-booking and instantly generating digital work orders.
4. **Digital Production Lot Passport**: The signature differentiator — an auditable, database-backed operational ledger recording submitted vs physically received yardage, production milestone stages, defect inspections, rework loops, and final delivery sign-offs.

## 3. Geographical Focus
- **Primary Market**: Surat, Gujarat (India’s largest man-made fabric and synthetic textile trading hub, comprising Pandesara GIDC, Sachin GIDC, Udhna GIDC, Anjana Farm, and the Ring Road wholesale market clusters).
- **Expansion Horizon**: Ahmedabad, Jetpur, Tirupur, Ichalkaranji, and Pan-India textile clusters.

## 4. Key Target Personas
- **Buyer Persona**: Wholesale fabric merchants, apparel brands, boutique fashion houses, and garment exporters who outsource fabric processing.
- **Job-Worker Persona**: Independent mill owners, processing houses, digital printing workshops, and contract garment stitching units seeking steady machine utilization.
- **Admin Persona**: Platform moderators responsible for verifying trade licenses, monitoring dispute resolutions, managing categories, and exporting operational audit reports.
