## 2. Customer Value Architecture

### 2.1 Value Delivered to Buyers (Traders & Brands)
1. **Search & Evaluation Cost Reduction**: Reduces the time required to source verified processing partners from 3 days to under 30 minutes.
2. **Pricing Transparency**: Buyers receive apples-to-apples breakdowns of base processing rates, shrinkage allowances, and expected turnarounds.
3. **Risk Mitigation (The Lot Passport)**: Eliminates finger-pointing by binding raw fabric inward counts and stage milestones to an immutable timeline.

### 2.2 Value Delivered to Service Providers (Job-Workers)
1. **Capacity Optimization**: Mills can broadcast open soft-flow jet capacity during off-peak windows, minimizing idle operating fixed costs.
2. **Disintermediation of Brokers**: Reclaims the 1.5%–3.0% commission traditionally extracted by informal middlemen who provide no operational value.
3. **Protection Against Baseless Claims**: Digital recording of raw fabric receipts establishes proof if delivered fabric arrives pre-stained, torn, or under-metered.

## 3. Financial Viability & Unit Economics

### Projected Unit Economics per Completed Job Order (Representative 5,000-Meter Batch)
- **Average Job Order Processing Value**: ₹1,25,000 (assuming ₹25/meter dyeing & finishing).
- **Marketplace Take-Rate (1.0%)**: ₹1,250 net revenue per transaction.
- **Cost of Servicing (Cloud hosting, notification SMS, payment gateway API)**: ~₹120.
- **Contribution Margin per Order**: ~₹1,130 (**~90.4% Gross Margin**).
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Assuming an active textile trader processes 4 orders per month on TexSetu over a 24-month horizon:  
  $$LTV = 4 \times 24 \times ₹1,130 = ₹1,08,480$$
- **Estimated Blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: ~₹8,500 (via direct association partnerships and field onboarding).
- **LTV:CAC Ratio**: **~12.7:1**, indicating exceptional unit economic leverage typical of high-frequency B2B vertical platforms.
