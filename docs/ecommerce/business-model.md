# E-Commerce Business Model & Revenue Strategy — TexSetu

## 1. Marketplace Archetype: Vertical B2B Job-Work Coordination

TexSetu operates as a **two-sided vertical B2B marketplace and workflow coordination platform**. Unlike B2C e-commerce (e.g., Amazon, Flipkart) where standardized finished inventory is purchased off the shelf, B2B textile job-work involves customized processing services performed on raw material owned by the buyer.

### Marketplace Classification
- **Inventory Model**: Zero-inventory (Asset-light). TexSetu neither owns raw fabric nor operates processing machinery.
- **Contract Model**: Principal-to-Principal contract coordination between registered textile buyers (merchants, apparel brands) and verified job-workers (dyeing, printing, stitching units).
- **Core Unit of Value**: The digital facilitation, auditable custody tracking, and dispute-free settlement of custom job-work lots.

## 2. Multi-Tiered Revenue Model

TexSetu combines transaction commission fees with value-added SaaS subscriptions to create resilient, counter-cyclical revenue streams:

```

                                             │
         ┌───────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┐
         │                                   │                                   │
         ▼                                   ▼                                   ▼
 [Transaction Take Rate]            [Job-Worker SaaS Tiers]            [Value-Added Services]
  0.75% to 1.5% GMV on               Monthly / Annual Plans             • Express Dispute Audit
  completed work orders.             (Verified Pro & Enterprise).       • Chemical Testing Ties
                                                                        • Batch Analytics API
```

### 2.1 Transaction Commission (Take-Rate)
- **Fee Structure**: 0.75% to 1.25% charged to the buyer and 0.5% charged to the job-worker upon successful order completion and inspection acceptance.
- **Why Users Willingly Pay**:
  - Traditional offline brokers extract **1.5% to 3.0%** in commission without providing any tracking, inspection records, or legal recourse.
  - TexSetu reduces transaction cost by more than 50% while delivering complete digital traceability.

### 2.3 Financial & Value-Added Services (Roadmap Horizon)
- **Invoice Financing Referral**: Partnering with digital NBFCs to provide job-workers early payment discounting against completed, buyer-accepted Lot Passports.
- **Accredited Testing Partnerships**: Referral commissions for linking textile batch samples to certified testing laboratories (SASMIRA, ATIRA) for color fastness and tensile strength verification.

## 3. Marketplace Defensibility & Network Effects

1. **Two-Sided Cross-Network Effects**: More verified dyeing and printing mills in Pandesara attract more merchant traders from the textile markets; higher RFQ liquidity incentivizes mills to keep their capacity status updated.
2. **High Switching Costs via the Lot Passport Ledger**: As a job-worker accumulates verified order history, high inspection ratings, and documented turnaround metrics, their TexSetu profile becomes their primary commercial resume, creating immense platform loyalty.
3. **Data Moat on Processing Yields**: Over thousands of transactions, TexSetu builds benchmark intelligence on expected shrinkage and processing yields per fabric type (e.g., standard shrinkage for 60g Georgette vs. 80g Crepe), enabling predictive risk scoring.
