Step-by-Step Workflow

How TexSetu Operates

A transparent, transactional workflow designed for textile traders and specialized job-work units.

For Textile Traders & Fabric Buyers

Step 1
Post an RFQ

Specify processing category (dyeing, printing, stitching), fabric specs, quantity, and required delivery date.

Step 2
Compare Quotations

Receive competitive bids from verified Surat units. Compare unit rate, turnaround days, and minimum quantities.

Step 3
Accept & Track

Accept one quotation. A Digital Production Lot Passport is initialized to track material arrival and processing.

Step 4
Inspect & Sign Off

Review quality reports, approve completed work or request rework if defects occur, and confirm final delivery.

For Specialized Job-Work Units

Step 1
List Capabilities

Showcase your machinery (stenters, jet dyeing, digital printers), MOQ, turnaround days, and indicative rates.

Step 2
Bid on Market RFQs

Browse open enquiries from reputable traders and submit competitive quotations directly.

Step 3
Update Milestones

Acknowledge fabric receipt, update production stages (processing, quality check), and upload evidence.

Step 4
Deliver & Build Trust

Dispatch finished goods, resolve any rework requests smoothly, and accumulate transaction-linked reviews.
