Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

TexSetu collects business registration details, business contact phone numbers, GST/PAN credentials for verification, technical processing specifications in RFQs, quotation prices, and operational production lot events.

2. Usage of Data

Your business data is strictly utilized to facilitate supplier discovery, quotation exchange, order management, and Digital Production Lot Passport maintenance. We never sell or share commercial order specifications with unauthorized third parties.

3. Security & Access Control

All passwords are cryptographically hashed using standard BCrypt encoders. Commercial quotations and work order records are restricted by role-based authorization to only the transacting buyer, selected service provider, and platform administrators.

4. Verification Records

Documents uploaded for job-worker verification are stored in secured storage buckets and reviewed only by authorized administrators.
