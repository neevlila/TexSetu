Our Mission

Organizing Surat's Textile Job-Work Ecosystem

TexSetu ("Textile Bridge") is a specialized B2B marketplace created to bridge the operational gap between fabric traders, clothing brands, and specialized job-work manufacturing units in Surat, Gujarat.

The Problem Hypothesis

Textile businesses in hubs like Pandesara, Sachin, and Ring Road manage hundreds of thousands of meters of fabric across fragmented communication channels — phone calls, WhatsApp receipts, paper challans, and manual spreadsheets. Misaligned turnaround estimates, unverified capacity claims, quality disputes, and missing audit trails create costly delays and material waste.

The TexSetu Approach

TexSetu introduces a digital workflow engineered specifically for textile job-work:

Capability-Based Discovery

Search providers by proven machinery, certified GIDC facilities, and realistic daily capacities.

Quotation Comparison

Side-by-side matrices comparing unit rates, turnaround days, and delivery terms before commitment.

Digital Lot Passport

Every work order maintains an auditable digital timeline from fabric receipt to final dispatch.

Quality & Rework Loops

Recorded defect inspections trigger explicit rework stages rather than unrecorded verbal disputes.

Academic & Startup Profile

TexSetu is architected using modern enterprise standards (Java 21, Spring Boot 3.3, PostgreSQL, Spring Security 6) to demonstrate a production-ready, cloud-deployable platform suitable for startup incubation under DPIIT Startup India guidelines.
